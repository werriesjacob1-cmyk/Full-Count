How to read Full Count

Probability
Full Count's estimated chance the bet wins.
Edge
The difference between Full Count's probability and what the sportsbook's price implies.
Top Pick
Meets Full Count's full recommendation standard — real probability, evidence, price, and freshness, all at once.
Lean
Full Count's data favors a side, but it isn't an official recommendation.
Value
The price pays more than the win probability alone would justify — a different claim than "likely to win."
Evidence Quality
How much real track record backs this read — more games/starts observed means a stronger grade.
